# Q25 Publish Healthcare Report to Power BI Service
1. Desktop: Home > **Publish** > sign in with work/school account > choose workspace.
2. Service (app.powerbi.com): Workspaces > **New workspace** `Healthcare Analytics` (add description, Pro/PPU/Fabric license). Publish into it; the workspace now has the **Semantic model** and the **Report** (organise with a naming convention, e.g. `HC_Operations_Model`).
3. Open the report > Edit/Reading view > hover a visual > **Pin** (pin icon) > New dashboard `Hospital Operations Dashboard`. Pin several visuals/live page. Rename tiles. Share to authorised employees: Share > add emails, or use an App (Q28).
